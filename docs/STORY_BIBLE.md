# Ninth Tide — Story Bible v1.0

## Creative mandate
A 10–15-hour **total-content** dark-fantasy investigation visual novel: ~8–10 hours on an initial complete route plus 4–6 hours of meaningful alternate content. Ten endings, nine main chapters plus prologue and finale. This is a writing target, not achieved runtime.

**Premise:** Every ninth tide the Sunken Castle rises, carrying the people and memories of a kingdom erased from maps. The player awakens with four lives, an empty name, and a royal death warrant bearing their signature. Each death returns them to a bell's toll, but memories and relationships do not reset cleanly.

**Core question:** Is a life saved by stealing another person's future truly saved?

**Tone:** Baroque maritime mystery, wit under pressure, emotionally grounded characters, moral ambiguity, shocking but fair reveals. Keep the logic of clues and witness statements rigorous.

No cast member is a simple exposition dispenser. Each needs at least one private objective, a contradictory belief, a reveal, a reversible and an irreversible consequence, and distinct physical mannerisms.

## Act and chapter map
- **Prologue — The Body on the Shore:** The player finds their own corpse. Tutorial: inspect, testimony, choice, first life counter.
- **1 — Harbour of Lost Names:** Mara, Cassian, Talia. A witness changes testimony after the bell. Introduce trust and suspicion.
- **2 — The Masquerade Murder:** Vesper and Isolde. Locked-room killing, costume swaps, first formal evidence confrontation.
- **3 — Library of False Memories:** Elias. Compare two editions of a decree, discover erased citizens and altered dates.
- **4 — Trial Beneath the Sea:** Seraphine and Lucien. Multi-round courtroom-style testimony, contradiction chains, costly objections.
- **5 — Engine That Eats Tomorrow:** Nerissa. Physical machine investigation, time-offset clues, permanent world-state fork.
- **6 — The Saint and the Sinner:** Oren and Isolde. False miracle, medical evidence, faith-versus-truth conflict.
- **7 — The King's Unspoken Crime:** Aldren, Corvin. Political history, witness betrayal, faction commitment.
- **8 — Four Lives, One Truth:** Cross-case timeline board, identity reveal, life-dependent alternate solutions.
- **Finale — When the Ninth Tide Falls:** Branching resolution, unique ending scenes and epilogues.

## Endings and prerequisites (design intent)
1. **The Tenth Dawn:** resolve the cycle by freeing the bound memories; requires a complete proof chain.
2. **Crown of Salt:** take Aldren's place; requires sovereign bargain and political leverage.
3. **Mara's Last Oath:** choose Mara over the city; requires deep loyalty and a rescue decision.
4. **The Archivist's Truth:** publish the original records; requires Elias trust and unaltered archives.
5. **The Final Masquerade:** rewrite the city's identity with Vesper; requires masked-route clues.
6. **The Drowned God:** surrender self to the tide; requires high tide affinity and accepting its voice.
7. **The Iron Verdict:** impose uncompromising order; requires legal victory and Seraphine/Lucien alignment.
8. **A Kingdom Forgotten:** erase the engine's memory store; requires sabotage evidence and moral sacrifice.
9. **The Ninth Sacrifice:** expend final life to release others; requires sacrifice opportunity.
10. **The Unwritten Sea:** secret true ending; requires the full evidence chain, three cross-route revelations, and a surviving life.

Every ending must have a distinct confrontation, decision, aftermath, and at least one character-specific epilogue.

## Investigation mechanics
- Each case has rooms, examinable hotspots, evidence cards, witness statements, and a reconstruction board.
- Evidence has provenance, reliability, tags, acquisition conditions, and optional contradiction targets.
- Witness testimony is line-by-line. Presenting wrong evidence has a consequence (reputation, life, or case pressure), never random failure.
- Key deductions have a fair clue trail and a fallback so one missed optional item does not softlock the game.
- Journal records claims and unresolved contradictions separately; do not reveal solutions before earned.
- Major choices modify trust, suspicion, loyalty, faction standing, lives, Resolve, Secrets, Tide, and permanent flags.

## Visual and audio direction
Original high-contrast cel-shaded anime art with thick black contours, expressive face variants, sharply posed silhouettes, detailed layered environments, dynamic impact cuts, and pointed speech-bubble UI. Reference courtroom-game theatrical readability, never copy franchise sprites, logos, or characters.

Narrator: original refined British female delivery, low-mid register, cool and measured. Use a licensed actor/voice model or user-selected British TTS fallback; do not clone an existing performance.

## Writing and acceptance
Target ~85k–125k unique authored words across routes (estimate; actual pacing must be measured). Each chapter needs a beat outline, scene script, evidence logic, route variations, continuity audit, and playtest. Track unique words and estimated reading duration separately. No fake progress based solely on scaffold files.
